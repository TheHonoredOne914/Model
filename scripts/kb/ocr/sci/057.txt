Bones have a rigid matrix containing calcium and phosphorus
compounds, giving them strength and rigidity. In contrast, cartilage has
a soft, jelly-like matrix, and provides flexibility and cushioning. Other
connective tissues include tendons and ligaments. Tendons connect
muscles to bones, while ligaments connect bones to bones and prevent
excessive movement (Fig. 3.12c).
