Sexual reproduction in plants has an applied importance in plant breeding. There are several
methods of plant breeding, such as selective breeding, artificial hybridisation, genetically
engineered crops, and more. In selective breeding, farmers select plants of desirable characters for

duction. Process of artificial hybridisation involves removal of the stamens from the flowers,
covering it with a bagging to prevent self-pollination, and manual transfer of the pollens with
desired characters. Genetic engineering is also applied by inserting the genetic material of desired
characters into the DNA of the selected varieties. This leads to the development of new varieties,
such as high yielding varieties, disease resistant varieties, and so on. This has revolutionised crop

agriculture.
