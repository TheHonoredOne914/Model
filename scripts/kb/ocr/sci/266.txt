Rules for writing the scientific names
1. The name has two parts — genus and species.
2. The genus name begins with a capital letter and comes first, followed
by the species name, which is written in small letters (lower case).

3. The scientific name is written in italics when printed, or underlined
when handwritten (Fig. 12.18).
