You might have observed many types of crystals in everyday life, such
as rock salt crystals (Fig. 5.7a) or sugar crystals (Fig. 5.7b) that grow while

formed when water vapour freezes in the air. Even frost on windows is
formed when water vapour turns into ice crystals (Fig. 5.7c). This is how
crystals form naturally.
