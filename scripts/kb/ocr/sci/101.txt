A mixture of acetone and water can be separated by distillation because
their boiling points differ sufficiently. Acetone boils at about 56 °C and
water boils at 100 °C. A large difference in boiling points allows one liquid
to vaporise before the vapours of the other liquid are formed in significant
amount.

The process of distillation has been used for a long time for
various purposes, including extraction of fragrances from
flowers to make perfumes.

rises from the ground after the first rain. In Kannauj, a
town in Uttar Pradesh known as the perfume capital of
India, this beautiful fragrance is captured and turned into
a natural perfume called Mitti ka Ittar (earthy fragrance).
