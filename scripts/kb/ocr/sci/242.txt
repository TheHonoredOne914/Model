11.5.8 Mother's health during pregnancy

A woman's health during pregnancy plays a vital role in the baby’s growth
and safety. She should eat a balanced diet rich in proteins, vitamins and
minerals, attend regular medical check-ups, and follow her doctor's
advice regarding light exercise and rest. Her emotional well-being is also
important, and support from family members helps her remain healthy
and stress-free.
