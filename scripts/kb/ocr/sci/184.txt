Experimental set-up 2

1, Place a clean, dry 100 mL conical flask and a
medium-sized balloon on a weighing balance.

2. Set the balance reading to zero by pressing the tare or
reset button.

3. Pour about 20 mL of vinegar or lemon juice into the
conical flask.

4. Place about 2g of baking soda (sodium
hydrogencarbonate) in the balloon.

5. Fix the balloon to the mouth of the conical flask using
a thread, without allowing the baking soda to mix
with the vinegar.

6. Weigh the conical flask containing vinegar and the
balloon containing baking soda, and record the
