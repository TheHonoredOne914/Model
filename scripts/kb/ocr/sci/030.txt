If the estimated size of an onion peel cell is 200 pm, how much does a
light microscope magnify this cell? The total magnification of a microscope
depends on its magnifying power of the eyepiece and the objective lens. If
both the eyepiece and the objective lens have the magnifying power of 10X,
then the total magnification will be 100X. This means that a cell with an
estimated size of 200 um will appear 100 times larger.
