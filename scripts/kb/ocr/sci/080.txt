Example 4.6: What does the graph shown in Fig. 4.15 indicate about the
nature of motion of the vehicle?

Answer: The position of the vehicle is 40 m from the origin and is
not changing with time. Thus, the vehicle is at rest at 40 m from the
origin. A straight line parallel to the time axis on a position-time graph
represents a stationary object (In this case, the position-time graph is not

distance-time graph).
