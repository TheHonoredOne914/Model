10.1.1 Tuning fork

An instrument that is often used for experiments with sound is a tuning
fork. A tuning fork is a U-shaped metal bar with a stem. It is usually
made of steel or aluminium. The sides of the ‘U’ are called prongs or tines
(Fig. 10.4a) which are struck on a pad to make them vibrate.

10.2 Propagation of Sound

You have seen that sound is produced by vibrating objects. How does sound
reach your ear from the source? Sound travels through air but does it also
travel through solids and liquids?
