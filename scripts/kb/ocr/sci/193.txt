Cations and anions are collectively called ions. Once the sodium and
chloride ions are formed, they are held together by the electrostatic force
of attraction due to their opposite charges (Fig. 9.13). The electrostatic force
of attraction between oppositely charged ions that holds them together is
called an ionic bond.
