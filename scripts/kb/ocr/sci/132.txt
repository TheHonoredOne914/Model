* Newton's first law of motion: An object at rest remains at rest, and an
object in motion continues to move with a constant velocity, unless a
net force acts upon the object.

* Newton's second law of motion; When a net force acts on an object,
the object accelerates in the direction of the net force. The magnitude
of the acceleration is proportional to the magnitude of the net force
and is inversely proportional to the mass of the object.

* Newton's third law of motion: Whenever one object is exerting a
force ona second object, the second object is simultaneously exerting
an equal and opposite force on the first object.
