Did you notice (Fig. 10.9) that the displacement of the medium is parallel
to the direction of wave propagation? In sound waves, the particles of the
medium vibrate back and forth parallel to the direction of propagation
of disturbance (Fig. 10.12). Such waves where the particles vibrate in a
direction parallel to the direction of the wave propagation are known as
longitudinal waves.

Fig. 10.12: A longitudinal wave

If there is no medium (ie., no particles), is the propagation of sound
waves possible? Without a material medium, sound waves cannot
propagate. Waves that require a material medium for propagation are
called mechanical waves. Sound wave is a type of a mechanical wave.
