and occurs only in the cells of reproductive organs.

Errors in mitosis lead to uncontrolled cell divisions.
This can lead to the formation of tumours and abnormal
number of chromosomes in body cells.

Errors in meiosis may result in genetic disorders,
which may be associated with developmental problems
or distinctive physical features. Faulty meiosis may also
cause early pregnancy loss or reduced fertility.
