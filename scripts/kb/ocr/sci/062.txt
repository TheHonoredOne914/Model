From one cell to an organism: Totipotency
In 1958, F. C. Steward demonstrated that even single cells from vascular
phloem of carrot retain the ability to regenerate whole plants. He was the first
person to do so!
He and his team conducted an experiment using the phloem cells of
carrot. He grew these cells in a nutrient medium containing simple sugars and
hormones under appropriate conditions. He observed that these phloem cells
divided to form a mass of cells, which gradually divided and differentiated
into a complete plant.
