In real world, the conditions for uniform circular motion — constant
speed and a circular path — are often not met. So, uniform circular motion
is an idealised model of the real-world situations. Still, it is a useful model
as it serves as the foundation for more complex real-world situations,
such as motion of planets revolving around the Sun or a vehicle making a
circular turn.
