Carbon constitutes ~ 49% of dry weight of living organisms. Out of the total quantity of global
carbon, 71% carbon is found in oceans. This oceanic reservoir regulates the amount of carbon
dioxide in the atmosphere.

Do you know that the atmosphere holds only a very small fraction (about 1%) of the total
global carbon?
