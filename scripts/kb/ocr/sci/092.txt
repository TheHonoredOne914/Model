Chapter

Exploring Mixtures
and their Separation

Have you ever wondered how sweet, white crystals of sugar are
obtained from tall, green sugarcane plants? Or how do doctors
detect diseases like malaria using just a few drops of blood?
Many such everyday activities are made possible by techniques
based on the fascinating science of separating mixtures.

Grasse You have learnt about mixtures and some simple
methods to separate them. In this chapter, you will
explore mixtures in greater depth, including their
properties, behaviour and the various techniques used to
separate them. From industrial processes like sugar production
to life-saving medical tests, the separation of mixtures plays a
crucial role in our daily lives.
