Future expectations

If producers or suppliers expect a boom in the demand for goods:
in the near future, they will produce more, and supply will rise. :
Similarly, if the producer is expecting lower demand, they will
reduce production, leading to a fall in supply. For instance, if potato
wholesalers expect prices to rise during the peak season, they might
hold back supply now to sell later at higher prices.
