Fig. 4.31, The Step Pyramid at Saqqara

Over time, powerful individuals called ‘Pharaohs’ emerged as the
rulers of Egypt. After their death, these pharaohs were buried deep
underground and arectangular structure called a mastaba was placed
over the burial chamber. Gradually, these mastabas were placed one
on top of the other to form a pyramid. One prominent example of
such pyramids is the step pyramid at Saqqara. The pyramids were
built primarily because the Egyptians believed that each person had
a ka (a spiritual double) that lived on after death if the body was
preserved through mummification.
