How was the Egyptian script deciphered?

Pierre Bouchard, a French army engineer, while repairing a fort

in Egypt in 1799, found a giant black stone covered in mysterious

writing. This was the Rosetta Stone, which had three types of
| writing, including Greek. Since people could still read Greek, this
_ was a huge breakthrough — like having a bilingual dictionary
for a lost language. In 1822, a French linguist, Jean-Francois
Champollion, finally deciphered the script.
