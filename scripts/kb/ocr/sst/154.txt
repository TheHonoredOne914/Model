Delimitation Commission

Did you know that the boundaries of your parliamentary and
assembly constituencies are not permanently fixed? The boundaries
of the constituencies change over time depending on shifts in the
population density. Without delimitation, one Member of Parliament
(MP) might represent five lakh people, while another might represent
twenty-five lakh, defeating the objective of equal representation.
Delimitation is the process of determining the number of seats and
fixing the territorial boundaries in each state for elections to the Lok
Sabha and the Vidhan Sabha. It aims to ensure that the ratio of seats
to population is as close to equal as possible across all constituencies.
