THINK ABOUT IT

Fig. 4.20. Stone weights

Harappans had their own standard systems of weight and
measurement. This is evident from the meticulously planned
settlements and efficient interregional trade practices of the
Harappans. They followed a binary multiple (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, etc.)
system for weighing smaller units and used multiples of ten for
larger denominations. Such cubical stone weights have been
reported from several sites. Can you imagine how long-distance
trade would have been affected had the Harappans not followed a
standard system of weights?
