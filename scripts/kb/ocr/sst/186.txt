Other Determinants of Demand

When a new model of a popular smartphone is launched, long
queues and pre-bookings indicate the rush to buy it, even if it is more
expensive. So, the demand for a product does not necessarily change
only because of price. Many other factors influence how much people
want to buy, even when the price of the good or service remains the
same. Let us see some of these factors at play.
