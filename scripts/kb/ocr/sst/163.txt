The Anti-Defection Law in India was passed in 1985 through the
52nd Constitutional Amendment Act. The purpose of the law is
to prevent defection, thereby providing stability to the elected
government. According to this law, if a member of the Parliament
or a State Assembly voluntarily gives up their party membership
or votes against the party's instructions (whip), the member
can be disqualified from the respective House. The Speaker
or Chairman of the House decides what needs to be done in

such cases.
