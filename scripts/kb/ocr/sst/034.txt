Sea breeze

It is a local wind that blows from sea to land
during the day, especially in the afternoon,
when the land becomes relatively warmer
than the sea. This results in the formation of
a low-pressure region over land, and so the
wind starts blowing from the sea.

Land breeze

It is a local wind that blows from land to sea
during the night, resulting from differential
surface cooling between the land and the sea.
Land cools faster than sea at night. Since the
difference in temperatures and air pressure
between the sea and the land is low, the wind
speed is low.
