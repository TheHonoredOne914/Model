Climate change is the long-term change in weather caused
mainly by human activities like burning fuels and cutting trees,
which leads to global warming and extreme weather conditions.

We can reduce our carbon footprint by saving energy, using
renewable sources, planting trees, and living in more eco-
friendly ways.
