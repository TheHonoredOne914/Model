Sindhu-Sarasvati Civilisation

In the previous section, you have seen how the Neolithic way of
life that emerged around 7000 BCE at Mehrgarh in the foothills of
Baluchistan, spread in the middle and upper Indus valley and further
east. Some of these settlements mastered the extraction of copper
from its ores around 4000 BCE, and became the earliest Chalcolithic
sites in the subcontinent marking the beginning of the Bronze Age.
